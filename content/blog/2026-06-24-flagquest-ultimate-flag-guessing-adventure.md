Embark on a global journey from the comfort of your mobile device! Whether you are a dedicated geography buff, a casual trivia player, or someone looking to brush up on international flags, **FlagQuest** is built for you. 

FlagQuest is a vibrant, interactive mobile game designed to take players on an educational and competitive adventure across all **195 nations**. By combining fast-paced quiz mechanics, dynamic game modes, pass-and-play multiplayer, and smart educational elements, FlagQuest transforms the process of learning flags into an addictive, premium gaming experience.

---

## 🎮 Diverse Game Modes

FlagQuest adapts to your preferred playstyle. The game features four distinct gameplay modes designed to test your knowledge, build confidence, or spark competition with friends:

### 🏆 Classic Mode
The perfect entry point for explorers. In Classic Mode, players are shown a flag and must choose the correct country from four multiple-choice options.

*   **Lives System:** You start with **3 lives**. One wrong guess costs a life.
*   **Hints:** Access up to **3 hints** per game to help you out of tight spots.
*   **Goal:** Answer as many questions as you can and achieve the ultimate high score before running out of lives.

### ⚡ Time Attack
An adrenaline-pumping race against the clock where speed is just as important as accuracy.

*   **Customizable Starting Time:** Adjust your initial timer (e.g., 30s, 60s, or 90s) in the game settings to set your pace.
*   **Dynamic Time Rewards:** Correct answers add precious seconds (**+2s**) to your clock, while incorrect guesses penalize you (**-5s**).
*   **Goal:** Survive as long as possible under intense time pressure and push your reaction times to the limit.

### ♾️ Endless Mode
A relaxed, stress-free practice sandbox environment perfect for learning at your own pace.

*   **No Limits:** No timers, no lives, and no game-over screens.
*   **Focus:** Keep playing as long as you want to build consecutive answer streaks and familiarize yourself with the world's most obscure and intricate flags.

### 👥 Local Multiplayer ("Pass & Play")
Bring the geography battle to your friends and family directly on a single device!

*   **Custom Setup:** Supports **2 to 4 players** and a customizable number of rounds.
*   **How it works:** A turn-based system where players take turns guessing their flags. The clean, intuitive app UI guides players on when it's their turn and when to "pass the phone" to the next player.
*   **Goal:** Outscore your opponents and claim the ultimate title of Flag Master on the post-match podium scoreboard.

## 🌟 Immersive Gameplay Features

FlagQuest is packed with user-first design and educational features that make the app feel rewarding and alive.

### 💡 Interactive "Fun Fact" Clues
Stuck on a tricky flag? Instead of guessing blindly, players can use one of their hints to activate the interactive card flip. 

With a smooth, responsive 3D animation, the flag card flips over to reveal a fascinating, educational trivia fact about the country's culture, geography, or history. These curated clues help you logically deduce the correct answer. You can flip the card back and forth as many times as you need before locking in your choice.

### 📈 Career & Progress Tracking
Track your achievements and watch your knowledge grow. FlagQuest automatically stores your high scores for each game mode. 

In the player profile settings, you can view your custom username alongside a personalized explorer title (such as *"Explorer of 195 nations"*). Your title dynamically upgrades as you learn more flags, representing your progress toward becoming a global geography expert.

### 🔊 Premium Sensory Design
FlagQuest is crafted with modern, tactile, and highly responsive aesthetics:

*   **Audio & Haptic Feedback:** Enjoy satisfying sound effects and premium tactile vibrations for correct and incorrect answers (both of which can be customized or disabled in the settings menu).
*   **Vibrant Visual Themes:** Adaptive light and dark modes that coordinate with your system preferences, complete with physics-based card flips and color transitions.

## ⚙️ Fully Customizable Experience

Tailor your geography adventure to suit your preferences:

*   **Explorer Profile:** Customise your explorer name in the profile section.
*   **Time Attack settings:** Adjust the start timer count to fine-tune the difficulty.
*   **Audio toggles:** Turn sound effects and haptic vibrations on or off.
*   **Continent Filter:** Filter quiz questions by specific continents (Europe, Asia, North/South America, Africa, Oceania) or play with the full pool of all 195 nations.
