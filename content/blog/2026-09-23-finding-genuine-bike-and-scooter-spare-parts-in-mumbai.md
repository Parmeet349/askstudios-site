## Practical Maintenance Tips for Mumbai Riders

Maintaining a two-wheeler in Mumbai's coastal climate requires proactive vigilance. For a deeper breakdown of service intervals, consult the comprehensive [two-wheeler maintenance guide](https://jeetauto.in/blog/two-wheeler-maintenance-guide/).

- **Check Brakes Pre-Monsoon**: Mumbai rains wash dirt and road grit into brake calipers. Replace worn pads before metal touches the disc rotor.
- **Inspect Chain Slack Every 500 km**: Humidity and water splashing accelerate chain wear. Regular lubrication and timely sprocket replacement will save your transmission.
- **Replace Air Filters Every Service Cycle**: Coastal dust and salt air quickly clog paper filter elements, leading to sluggish acceleration and poor fuel economy.
